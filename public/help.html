Getting Started

EasyMark is a local-first Markdown note app with AI assistance. Notes are saved as .md files in your Documents/EasyMark Notes/ folder.

Creating a Note

Click New Note in the sidebar or press Cmd/Ctrl+N. Type a title and press Enter.

Saving

Notes auto-save as you type. You can also press Cmd/Ctrl+S to manually save.

Markdown Editing

The editor works like Typora — it renders Markdown formatting inline as you type.

In a numbered or bullet list: press Enter for the next item, Shift+Enter to continue on a new line inside the same item, Tab / Shift+Tab to indent or outdent an item, or press Enter again on an empty item to leave the list and continue normal body text.

Inside a blockquote, press Enter to leave the quote or Shift+Enter to insert a line break within it.

Visual tables: click the table icon to insert a 3×3 table. Click a cell to show controls for adding or deleting rows and columns, changing the current column alignment, or deleting the table. Press Tab / Shift+Tab to move between cells; pressing Tab in the final cell creates a new row.

Folding: click the triangle in the left gutter of a heading to fold its section, or the triangle next to a parent list item to fold its nested list. Alt/Option+Click on the heading or parent item also toggles folding. The expand icon in the toolbar opens every folded block. Folding is visual only and never removes Markdown content.

AI Assistant

Click the robot icon in the title bar to open the AI panel. Before using AI, go to Settings → AI and enter your API key.

  • Write tab: Get suggestions or completions for selected text
  • Chat tab: Chat with AI about your note

You can also click Refresh in Settings → AI to fetch available models from your API.

Search & Replace

Press Cmd/Ctrl+F to open the search bar. Use Enter / Shift+Enter to navigate matches. Click Replace or All to replace text.

Images

Paste an image from your clipboard directly into the editor. Images are saved to the assets/ folder next to your notes.

Outline

Click the list icon in the editor toolbar to open the outline panel. Click any heading to jump to that section.

Settings

Click the gear icon in the title bar to open settings:

  • Appearance: Theme (light/dark), color scheme (Forest/Ocean/Terracotta/Monochrome), language
  • AI: API key, base URL, model selection with refresh
  • About: Version info

快速开始

EasyMark 是一款本地优先的 AI Markdown 笔记应用。笔记以 .md 文件保存在 Documents/EasyMark Notes/ 目录下。

新建笔记

点击侧边栏的「新建笔记」按钮或按 Cmd/Ctrl+N。输入标题后按回车确认。

保存

笔记自动保存。也可以按 Cmd/Ctrl+S 手动保存。

在有序或无序列表中：按 Enter 新建下一项；按 Shift+Enter 可在当前编号内换行并继续写正文；按 Tab / Shift+Tab 缩进或取消缩进；出现空编号后再按一次 Enter，即可退出列表并继续普通正文。

在引用块中，按 Enter 退出引用，按 Shift+Enter 在当前引用内换行。

可视化表格：点击工具栏的表格图标可插入一个 3×3 表格。点击单元格后会显示浮动工具栏，可添加或删除行列、调整当前列对齐方式，或者删除整个表格。按 Tab / Shift+Tab 可在单元格之间移动；在最后一个单元格按 Tab 会自动创建新行。

标题与列表折叠：点击标题左侧的三角箭头可折叠该标题下的内容；点击父列表项左侧的三角箭头可折叠其嵌套列表。也可以按住 Alt/Option 点击标题或父列表项。工具栏的展开图标可以一次展开全部内容。折叠仅影响显示，不会删除或改变 Markdown。

AI 助手

点击标题栏的「机器人图标」打开 AI 面板。首次使用需在「设置 → AI」中输入 API Key。

  • 写作：对选中文本获取建议或补全
  • 对话：与 AI 讨论你的笔记内容

在「设置 → AI」中点击「刷新」可从 API 拉取可用模型列表。

搜索替换

按 Cmd/Ctrl+F 打开搜索栏。Enter / Shift+Enter 上下导航。点击「替换」或「全部」进行替换。

图片

直接从剪贴板粘贴图片到编辑器中。图片自动保存到笔记同目录的 assets/ 文件夹。

大纲

点击编辑器工具栏的「列表图标」展开大纲面板。点击标题跳转到对应位置。

设置

点击标题栏的「齿轮图标」打开设置：

  • 外观：主题（亮/暗）、配色（森林/海洋/陶土/黑白）、语言
  • AI：API Key、地址、模型选择（支持刷新）
  • 关于：版本信息
